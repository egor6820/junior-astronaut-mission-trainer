# Lunar Outpost ECLSS Simulator

> **Interactive closed-loop Lunar Outpost ECLSS simulator built for the NASA Space Apps Challenge (*"Build a Junior Astronaut Mission Trainer"*).**

## About The Project

Space-themed STEM content often oversimplifies the engineering trade-offs that define a real mission or presents them at a level too complex to hold a young learner's attention. 

**Lunar Outpost ECLSS Simulator** bridges this gap by making those trade-offs both tangible and fun. Players step into the role of a mission commander managing a closed-loop Environmental Control and Life Support System (ECLSS) on the Moon, experiencing firsthand how resource management determines whether a mission succeeds or fails.

### Key Features
* **Closed-Loop Resource Management:** Monitor and recycle vital resources in real time.
* **Critical Engineering Trade-offs:** Balance competing demands across four core pillars:
  * **Power** generation and distribution
  * **Food** production
  * **Radiation** shielding
  * **Life Support** systems
* **Interactive Learning:** Designed to engage students and young learners through hands-on decision-making and immediate feedback.
